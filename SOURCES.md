# Sources — every headline on the page, as reported on 19 November 2026

**Lead — Trump, Xi & Putin hold first-ever three-way summit (Shenzhen).**
USA Today ("Trump, Putin, Xi hold historic three-way talks"), The Times
(live coverage + the joint declaration text), CBS News, BBC News, NDTV.
The three presidents met on the sidelines of the APEC forum in Shenzhen;
the talks lasted about 90 minutes. The joint statement — called a "peace
declaration" — pledges UN reform, dialogue on reducing nuclear risks
("a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought") and increased
production and supply of refined petroleum products. Ukraine was not
explicitly mentioned (USA Today; The Times).

**ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide.**
Just Security's Early Edition (19 Nov 2026) and Horizon's world briefing,
which made it the day's top story: the UN International Court of Justice
ruled that Myanmar committed state-sponsored genocide — the first ruling
of its kind by the court — in the case brought by Gambia over the
persecution of the Rohingya.

**Ukraine strikes the Crimean Bridge — third time this week.**
Russia said Ukraine attacked the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge for the third
time this week (Just Security, citing the day's reporting; BBC among the
outlets carrying it). Traffic across the Kerch Strait was suspended
(Nexus Europa). Putin vowed a "much more painful" response (The Times).

**Missiles over the Gulf.**
A New York Times reporter in Riyadh saw the Public Investment Fund Tower
damaged during an apparent Houthi missile attack (debris after an
interception) — via Just Security. Iran attacked Gulf states after US
strikes on Qeshm Island, and Israel launched a rare operation in central
Gaza (One Turn's day summary; Just Security).

**Anthropic's record IPO.**
News Media House's world desk: "Anthropic sets record with $81 billion
IPO at $1.5 trillion valuation", "surpassing SpaceX" as the biggest
stock-market debut. One Turn's summary likewise notes Anthropic "raised
a record amount in its stock market debut".

**COP31 final push in Antalya.**
Horizon: on Wednesday evening ministers met in informal session at COP31
in Antalya, Turkey, to overcome differences and adopt a final text;
Vanuatu urged reform of the climate talks as COP31 entered its final
phase (News Media House).

**Democrats take both houses of Congress.**
US midterm results: Democrats captured the House and the Senate
(LADbible/Independent reporting of Musk's reaction; New York Times via
10 Things). Hakeem Jeffries is set to become the first African-American
House Speaker (NDTV). Elon Musk, who spent $445m backing Republicans,
said he was "not MAGA" and called the spending foolish.
